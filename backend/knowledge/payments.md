# Payments and pricing

## How payment works
All payments are made online through Razorpay, a secure payment gateway. Razorpay supports common payment methods in India such as UPI, cards, net banking and wallets; the options you see are shown by Razorpay at checkout.

TurfBook never sees or stores your card or bank details. Payment details are entered only in the Razorpay checkout window.

## How the price is calculated
The total you pay has two parts:

1. Turf charges: the turf's price per hour multiplied by the number of hours you book, rounded to the nearest rupee. Each 30-minute slot costs half the hourly price.
2. Platform fee: 3% of the turf charges, rounded to the nearest rupee, with a minimum fee of Rs. 9.

Total = turf charges + platform fee. All amounts are in Indian rupees (INR).

## Price examples
- 1 hour at a turf costing Rs. 1,000 per hour: turf charges Rs. 1,000, platform fee Rs. 30 (3%), total Rs. 1,030.
- 2 hours at Rs. 1,200 per hour: turf charges Rs. 2,400, platform fee Rs. 72, total Rs. 2,472.
- 30 minutes at Rs. 200 per hour: turf charges Rs. 100. 3% would be Rs. 3, so the minimum fee of Rs. 9 applies. Total Rs. 109.

The amount is shown before you pay, and the same amount is charged by Razorpay.

## What is the platform fee?
The platform fee is TurfBook's service charge for running the booking platform. It is 3% of the turf charges, with a minimum of Rs. 9 per booking.

## Payment failed or checkout closed
If your payment fails or you close the Razorpay window without paying, the booking stays in "pending payment" and is not confirmed. No money is taken for a failed payment.

At the moment a pending booking is not released automatically, so that time may stay blocked for new bookings.

## Money was deducted but the booking is not confirmed
Sometimes the bank confirms a payment a little late. TurfBook also receives payment updates directly from Razorpay, so the booking is usually confirmed automatically within a short time. Refresh the turf page after a few minutes to check.

## Refunds
Refunds are not supported in the app yet. See the FAQ for cancellations.
